Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

1. Data handled by the application

The application stores locally on your Windows computer the account names, queued jobs, video metadata, logs and settings needed to operate the application.

2. OAuth credentials

OAuth tokens are stored in the operating system credential manager. They are used only to call the YouTube and TikTok APIs after you authorize the connected account. The application does not sell or share these credentials.

3. Platform APIs

When you connect an account or send a job, the relevant metadata and video file are sent directly to the platform selected by you. YouTube and TikTok process that information under their own privacy policies.

4. Retention and deletion

You can disconnect an account and remove local jobs, logs and settings from the application. Removing the application does not remove content already published on YouTube or TikTok.

5. Contact

Questions and privacy requests can be submitted through the project repository.
